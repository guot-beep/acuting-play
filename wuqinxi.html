How this works 玩法
  1. Master Xiong performs a sequence of animal forms — watch which cards light up. 熊師會示範一串動作，注意哪張卡亮起來。
  2. Then tap the same animals in the same order. 接著依照相同順序點出那些動物。
  3. Three rounds, each a little longer. Each animal trains one organ. 共三輪，一輪比一輪長。每一戲各養一臟。
