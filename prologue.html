Landmarks 定位標誌

  • 1
    The wrist crease — the line where the palm meets the forearm. Everything on the wrist is measured from here.腕橫紋——手掌與前臂交界的那條線。腕部取穴都從這裡算起。
  • 2
    The thumb side is called the radial side. The Lung channel runs down it.拇指那一側稱為橈側。肺經沿著它走下來。
  • 3
    Where the radial artery beats at the wrist crease — that is where we take the pulse.腕橫紋橈側、橈動脈搏動之處——就是我們診脈的地方。

Proportional Cun 骨度分寸

Measure the body by the body. Elbow crease to wrist crease is 12 cun on every patient, tall or small. 以身度身。肘橫紋到腕橫紋，人人皆為十二寸。
