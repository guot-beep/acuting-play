# AcuTing Play — 藥草卡插圖 Prompt（第二批）

四味藥：枸杞子、紅棗、當歸、茯苓
用途：Herb Codex 本草圖鑑卡面（正方形，遊戲內顯示約 190×190）

---

## 使用方式

1. 先把 **A · 共用風格區塊** 貼給 ChatGPT（只需貼一次）
2. 再貼 **B** 裡面某一味藥的段落
3. 生成後存成指定檔名，四張一起打包給我

**重要：一次只生成一味藥，不要一次生四張。** 一次多張的時候，AI 常常會把不同藥的特徵混在一起——乾薑那張配到紅色果實就是這樣來的。

---

## A · 共用風格區塊（每次都先貼這段）

> **最重要的一步：**貼這段的時候，同時附上 `herb-renshen.jpg`（人參）和
> `herb-aiye.jpg`（艾葉）當參考圖，跟 ChatGPT 說
> 「**match the style of these two exactly — same character cuteness,
> same paper, same background**」。
> 光靠文字描述很難把風格對齊，附參考圖成功率高很多。

```
Style: a collectible herb card illustration for a gentle Chinese-medicine
learning game. Aged ivory paper background (#F4EDDC) with a faint paper
grain. Soft grey ink-wash mountains and two or three thin outlined
auspicious clouds in the far background, very pale, never competing with
the subject. Muted palette only: warm beige, deep brown, sage green,
muted gold, soft terracotta — no saturated red or gold.

The subject is ONE cute anthropomorphic herb mascot, standing alone in
the centre of the frame, full body, in a light walking or waving pose.

Character look — match this closely, it is the house style:
- large round glossy black eyes with a bright highlight
- soft pink round blush circles on the cheeks
- a small open happy smile
- short stubby arms and legs growing straight out of the plant body
- often a small sage-green scarf, sash or leaf-cape as an accent
- warm, hand-painted, softly shaded — chubby and huggable

Cute, but hand-painted and slightly aged, not a glossy vector sticker.
A very fine pixel-dither texture over the painted forms. Soft even
light, gentle drop shadow on the ground.

Square 1:1 composition. A small terracotta seal stamp in one lower
corner. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels anywhere in the image.
No dragons, no lanterns, no glossy 3D, no photorealism.
```

## B · 四味藥

### 1 · 枸杞子 Goji Berry → 存成 `herb-gouqi.jpg`

```
Subject: a Lycium barbarum (goji) plant character.

Keep it as cute as the reference images, but the fruit shape must be
botanically right:
- The berries are SMALL, ELONGATED ovals — like a tiny rugby ball,
  roughly 1.5 cm — NOT round like a cherry or an apple.
- Colour is bright orange-red, slightly translucent.
- They hang in loose clusters from slender, arching, slightly thorny
  branches.
- Leaves are narrow and lance-shaped, soft green.
- Small five-petalled purple trumpet flowers may appear among them.

Character design: the body is a cluster of these small oblong berries
gathered on an arching branch, with narrow green leaves as arms and a
little leafy tuft on top. Big round glossy eyes, pink blush, happy open
smile. Cheerful, bouncy, light on its feet — the youngest of the group.
```

**必須是這樣：**細長橢圓、橘紅色、成串垂掛在細長枝條上、葉子窄長。
**不可以是：**單顆圓形紅果、蘋果狀、深紅色。

### 2 · 紅棗 Jujube → 存成 `herb-hongzao.jpg`

```
Subject: a Ziziphus jujuba (Chinese red date) character.

Botanical accuracy:
- ONE oval fruit, roughly 3 cm, clearly longer than it is wide.
- Dried jujube colour: deep brownish-red, with a distinctly WRINKLED,
  softly creased skin — this wrinkling is the signature and must be
  visible.
- The stem end has a small brown five-pointed calyx.
- Leaves are oval with three clear parallel veins running lengthwise,
  glossy green.

Character design: the wrinkled oval date is the body, with two small
three-veined leaves as arms and a short stem sprig on top. Big round
glossy eyes, pink blush, warm closed-eye smile. Round, soft and
grandmotherly — sturdier and calmer than the goji.
```

**必須是這樣：**單顆橢圓、深棕紅、表皮有明顯皺褶、葉子三出脈。
**不可以是：**光滑圓潤的紅果（那看起來就變成枸杞或蘋果了）。

### 3 · 當歸 Angelica Root → 存成 `herb-danggui.jpg`

```
Subject: an Angelica sinensis ROOT character — the medicinal part is the
root, so the root is the body.

Botanical accuracy:
- The root is pale yellowish-brown, fleshy, with a thick rounded crown
  at the top (歸頭) that divides below into several long tapering
  rootlets (歸尾) — this head-and-many-tails shape is the signature.
- The surface has fine horizontal wrinkles.
- Above the root, pinnate compound leaves and a rounded umbel of many
  tiny white flowers on a hollow stalk.

Character design: the pale root is the body, the several rootlets read
as legs and a flowing skirt, and the white flower umbel sits above like
a small parasol or bonnet. Big round glossy eyes, pink blush, a kind
gentle smile. Graceful and maternal — this is the herb of blood, so
give it a quiet warmth. Keep it cute, not elegant-adult.

Important: the root is PALE cream-brown, not the deep tan of ginseng,
and the flower head is a flat rounded cluster of tiny white flowers,
not a single blossom.
```

**必須是這樣：**淡黃棕色、上頭粗下面分多條細根尾、白色繖形花。
**不可以是：**像人參那樣的深褐色人形根（兩者很容易被畫混）。

### 4 · 茯苓 Poria → 存成 `herb-fuling.jpg`

```
Subject: a Poria cocos character. Note: poria is NOT a plant — it is a
fungus (a sclerotium) that grows underground on pine roots. Please do
not give it leaves or flowers.

Botanical accuracy:
- An irregular, lumpy, roughly potato-shaped mass, 10–20 cm.
- The outer skin is thin, dark greyish-brown, slightly cracked.
- Where it is broken open, the inside is soft matte WHITE — showing a
  little of this white interior is welcome, it is the herb's identity.
- It rests among the roots of a pine tree; a few pine roots and a
  scatter of pine needles nearby.

Character design: the lumpy brown-skinned body sits calmly among pine
roots, a small chip of white interior showing at one side like a patch.
Sleepy half-closed eyes, pink blush, a small content smile, stubby
little arms. Grounded and unhurried — this is the herb that quiets the
spirit, so it should look like it is about to doze off. Still cute and
huggable, just drowsy.
```

**必須是這樣：**不規則塊狀、外皮灰褐、內部白色、長在松根旁。
**不可以是：**有葉子或花的植物、蘑菇形狀的傘菌。

## C · 生成後的規格

| 項目 | 規格 |
|---|---|
| 比例 | 1:1 正方形 |
| 尺寸 | 1024×1024 就夠（我會壓到約 80 KB） |
| 格式 | JPG 或 PNG 都可以 |
| 檔名 | `herb-gouqi` / `herb-hongzao` / `herb-danggui` / `herb-fuling` |

打包成一個 zip 給我，我會壓縮、放進 `art/`、接上圖鑑，然後跑對應測試確認每張圖配到正確的藥。

## D · 檢查清單（丟給我之前先看一眼）

- [ ] 枸杞是**細長橢圓**，不是圓的
- [ ] 紅棗表皮有**皺褶**
- [ ] 當歸是**淡色的根**，不是深褐色人參
- [ ] 茯苓**沒有葉子**，是塊狀菌核
- [ ] 四張的紙底、山水、雲紋風格一致
- [ ] 圖上**沒有任何文字**
- [ ] 四隻都是**大眼睛 + 腮紅 + 圓身體**，跟人參、艾葉是同一家人

如果哪一張你覺得「好像不太對但說不上來」，那就先不要放上去——寧可先用文字印版，也不要教錯本草。
